# Caregiver reference artwork

Selected asset: Assets/Images/caregiver-holds-atlas.png. Original generated illustration atlas, 2172 x 724 pixels. Built-in image_gen was used; no external image, photograph or existing person's likeness was supplied. The selected solid-background version was generated fresh after rejecting an extra-hand variant and imperfect transparent cutouts. Discarded variants remain outside the project.

Final prompt: polished professional educational illustration sheet; three equal square panels on warm ivory; same fully clothed adult mother in an opaque high-neck cream sweater and sage trousers, and clothed doll in teal; cradle-inspired, cross-cradle-inspired and alongside placement references; natural proportions; exactly two adult arms and hands; no exposed breast, nipple, cleavage, abdomen, or intimate anatomy; no text or logos. The third image is labeled alongside placement rather than a clinically verified football hold.

The final atlas was visually inspected for faces, limb continuity, full clothing and obvious extra limbs before import. Each crop must also be checked in Preview. This does not establish clinical accuracy. Runtime cropping selects one third using UVs; the original file is not rewritten.

The final three Preview crops passed their capture scenarios and were visually inspected alongside the separate practice doll. Full-body in-view checks passed at the baseline and angled viewpoints.
