# Limits

- Simulation only. No real infant, camera recognition, breathing/swallowing/choking monitoring, sleepiness detection, or emergency response.
- No Specs hardware testing. Preview evidence is reported separately from hardware behavior.
- Head/hip height, head/body alignment, support distance, bottle contact, and incline are **demo geometry proxies**, not clinical measurements. All thresholds and guidance require clinician review.
- Head-above-hips is modeled for the approved shared demo registry. Its application to breast-feeding holds is unreviewed and is not a universal clinical rule.
- The 45-second pacing interval demonstrates a cue interaction. It is not a feeding schedule or a recommendation for real infant care.
- Surface type is a selected simulation category. Firmness is user confirmed. The Lens cannot establish that a real sleep surface is safe, compliant, or suitable.
- Crib mattress geometry uses the nominal 71 × 133 cm reference. Other surface sizes are illustrative demo parameters, not product specifications.
- Loose-object checks use oriented bounding volumes. They do not recognize physical objects or evaluate real bedding.
- Breast mode is a smaller placement guide. It does not assess latch, milk transfer, feeding effectiveness, or comfort.
- ASR is optional and off by default. Final-transcript parsing is unit tested; live microphone recognition and hardware voice behavior are not verified.
- Audio playback and the configured spatial path can be checked in Preview. Perceived direction and comfort on glasses remain unverified.
- No practice score is produced. Unknown input never becomes a passed check.
- Account-bound services, publishing, public repository hosting, CI execution on GitHub, and a published film are separate deliverables; none is implied by a local test result.
- The initial missing plan-lens skill and router sign-in gate were waived for local development. The official pack was later obtained and used for an explicitly post-build review; the original approved attachment is preserved. No earlier skill-pack interview or organizer acceptance is claimed.

- The clothed caregiver references are illustrations, not clinically verified latch/feeding diagrams. The third is labeled alongside placement. Sleep-facing tolerances and rendered-box containment remain simulation proxies.
- The official Devpost Learn template was applied after the initial build using prior user decisions and delegated final review. This does not backdate the original planning process or establish organizer acceptance.
- All 14 final Preview regressions and the complete rehearsal passed. Video/audio export remains a later phase; native audio sink warnings seen during refreshes must be checked in the recording pipeline.
